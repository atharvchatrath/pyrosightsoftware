Simulation only. The building, the smoke and the motion sensor are simulated. The AI model was trained on synthetic thermal images and has never seen a real fire, so treat its boxes as a demonstration of the pipeline, not of real-world accuracy. The green exit box is drawn where the device believes the doorway is, and only while its navigation estimate is trusted and the doorway is the next point on the way out.
